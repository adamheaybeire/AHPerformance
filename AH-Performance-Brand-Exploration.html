How to think about this: Palette A is your starting point at full strength. My take: Inter as the body font is a constant across all three — it's the most readable screen font available and works at every size. The real choice is the heading/brand font. Outfit (A) is the safest — clean and modern. DM Sans (B) is warmer and more approachable, great if you want clients to feel at ease. Space Grotesk (C) has the most character — it'd make AH Performance look like a premium digital product, not just another PT brand.
